Synthetic CRM case study

From an AI-proposed MERGE to a reviewable engineering plan.

This example shows the nine canonical planning deliverables generated for a synthetic CRM scenario. It demonstrates the structure, specificity and transparency of the handoff — not a production implementation.

← Return to the product overview

The scenario

Two accounts that may be related — but not identical.

An AI agent proposes merging two CRM account records based on several overlapping signals. A contract also suggests a possible parent or holding relationship. The example treats that as a material conflict to investigate, not as a detail to ignore.

Important interpretation notes

The example plan is explicitly marked not_implementation_ready. Eleven customer decisions remain blocking. Its 31 customer-operation scenarios are proposed tests; none was executed against a customer application. Draft 2020-12 JSON Schema and format validation were not run in the original generation environment. The plan does not decide legal identity, execute the merge or authorize a live operation. All names and account identifiers are synthetic.

What this example demonstrates

The connection between an investigation, an evidence-based recommendation, explicit customer-side controls, implementation responsibilities, validation scenarios and declared unknowns. The output is a starting point for customer engineering and policy review, not a turnkey production recipe.
